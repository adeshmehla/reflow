# Developer Profile — Instructions

Fill in the files below with real information. The chatbot will answer questions about you
only from this knowledge base, in the third person. It will say "not in the profile" for
anything not covered here.

Files to fill:
- 01-about.md       — name, title, location, brief bio
- 02-skills.md      — languages, frameworks, tools, years of experience
- 03-experience.md  — work history: company, role, dates, key achievements
- 04-projects.md    — notable projects: description, stack, your role, outcomes
- 05-education.md   — degrees, certifications, courses
- 06-contact.md     — LinkedIn, GitHub, email, portfolio URL

Keep each entry factual and specific (numbers, names, dates).
